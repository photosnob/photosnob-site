Description


World Exploration Cape Town Lungo honors South African affinity for intense coffee blends shaped by their coffee trade route history. Being a stopping point at the old trade routes of Asian coffees, Cape Town saw its taste shaped by the shipments of Robusta and Indian Arabicas that travelled through. They became the backbone of the country’s beloved intense coffee blends. Cape Town Lungo reflects that history with its potent blend of Indian Arabica and Robusta. The medium-roasted Washed Mexican Robusta brings a full body, whilst the dark-roasted Indian Arabicas lend a punchy bitter note and a characteristically Indian woodsy aroma. Savor our most intense Lungo like a Capetonian by adding milk to soften the brute strength of this flavor profile.
